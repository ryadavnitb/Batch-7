AirFly Insights is a data visualization and analytics project that analyzes large-scale airline flight data to understand **how airline operations work, why delays occur, and which factors influence performance**.

The project uses **Python for data preparation and exploratory analysis** and **Power BI for interactive dashboards**, delivering insights in a clear and structured manner.

---

## 🧭 Why AirFly Insights?

Airline delays affect passengers, airlines, and airport efficiency.  
This project answers practical questions such as:

- ✈️ Which airlines operate the most flights?
- 🛫 Which airports and routes are busiest?
- ⏱️ When do delays peak during the day or year?
- 🌦️ How much does weather impact delays?
- 📅 Do weekdays and weekends behave differently?

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Clean and prepare real-world airline datasets using Python  
- Engineer meaningful features for delay analysis  
- Perform exploratory data analysis (EDA)  
- Build structured, multi-page Power BI dashboards  
- Communicate insights through effective visual storytelling

## 🧪 Python-Based Data Analysis

📓 **Notebook:** `AirFly_Insights.ipynb`

Python was used as the **analytical backbone** of the project before dashboard creation.

### 🔹 Data Preparation
- Loaded and explored large airline, airport, and route datasets  
- Standardized column names and data formats  
- Handled missing and inconsistent values  
- Converted date and time fields into analyzable formats  

### 🔹 Feature Engineering
To support deeper analysis, several derived features were created:
- 🧭 Route (Origin → Destination)  
- 📅 Month, day, week, and season indicators  
- 🕒 Departure and arrival hour  
- 📆 Weekday vs weekend flag  
- 🚦 Delay categories based on total delay  
- 📏 Distance buckets and flight speed  
- 🌙 Red-eye flight indicator  

### 🔹 Exploratory Data Analysis
- Airline-wise and airport-wise traffic distribution  
- Identification of busiest routes and airports  
- Arrival and departure delay behavior  
- Cancellation and delay distribution analysis  
- Time-based patterns across hours, days, months, and seasons  

The processed dataset was then imported into **Power BI** for visualization.

## 📊 Power BI Dashboards (Final Report – 6 Pages)

---

### 📄 Page 1: Executive Overview

**Focus:** High-level operational snapshot

**Key Metrics**
- ✈️ Total Flights: ~6 Million  
- ⏱️ Average Delay: ~4.33 minutes  
- ❌ Cancellation Rate: ~0.02%  
- 🛫 Airports Covered: 322  
- 🏢 Airlines Covered: 15  

**Key Insight:**  
Overall airline operations are efficient with minimal cancellations.

---

### 📄 Page 2: Flight Operations Analysis

**Focus**
- Airline-wise flight volume  
- Busiest routes  
- Airport-level traffic  
- Monthly flight trends  

**Key Insights**
- A small group of airlines dominates total flight operations.  
- High-demand routes handle significant traffic.  
- Seasonal peaks are visible during mid-year months.  

---

### 📄 Page 3: Time-Based Trends & Delay Patterns

**Focus**
- Delays by month, hour, and day  
- Weekday vs weekend comparison  
- Seasonal delay behavior  

**Key Insights**
- Delay patterns vary noticeably across time periods.  
- Peak travel hours experience higher delays.  
- Weekdays account for the majority of flights.  

---

### 📄 Page 4: Airport Performance & Geographic Analysis

**Focus**
- Airport-level delay performance  
- Departure vs arrival delay relationship  
- Geographic distribution of airports  

**Key Insights**
- Major hub airports face higher congestion-related delays.  
- Departure delays strongly influence arrival delays.  

---

### 📄 Page 5: Weather Impact Analysis

**Focus**
- Weather-related delays by month and season  
- Contribution of weather delays to total delays  

**Key Insights**
- Weather delays increase during extreme seasonal conditions.  
- Weather is a significant but not exclusive cause of delays.  

---

### 📄 Page 6: Advanced Delay Insights

**Focus**
- Delay categories (Weather, Airline, Late Aircraft, Air System, Security)  
- Delay-prone airlines and airports  
- Route-level delay analysis  
- Weekday vs weekend flight distribution  

**Key Insights**
- Late aircraft and airline-related issues are major contributors.  
- High-traffic airports show higher delay percentages.  
- Business travel dominates weekday operations.  

---

## 🧠 Overall Insights

- Operational and congestion-related factors drive most delays.  
- Weather has a strong seasonal impact on performance.  
- Time of day, airline behavior, and airport traffic significantly affect delays.  
- Combining Python analysis with Power BI enables deeper understanding.
